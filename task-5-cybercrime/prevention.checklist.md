Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

1. Always verify the sender’s email address or phone number before clicking on any link.  
2. Do not click on suspicious or unknown links, especially those creating urgency or fear.  
3. Never share sensitive information like passwords, OTPs, or bank details through email or messages.  
4. Check the website URL carefully to ensure it is the official and secure (https) site.  
5. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on important accounts for added security.  
6. For UPI safety, never approve unknown payment requests or “collect requests” on apps like Google Pay or PhonePe.  
7. Do not scan unknown QR codes, as they may lead to fraudulent transactions.  
8. Regularly update your passwords and avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.  

If you become a victim of cybercrime, report immediately on the National Cyber Crime Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline number 1930.
