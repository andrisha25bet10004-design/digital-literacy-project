Phishing is a type of cybercrime where attackers try to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, or OTPs by pretending to be a trusted source. A common scenario involves fake emails or messages that appear to be from banks, online services, or even college authorities.

In a typical phishing case, a student receives an email claiming that their bank account will be blocked unless they verify their details immediately. The email includes a link that looks genuine but redirects to a fake website designed to look exactly like the bank’s official page. The student enters their login credentials and OTP, unknowingly giving access to the attacker. Within minutes, unauthorized transactions are made from the student’s account.

Phishing attacks often target students and young users because they are more active online and may not always verify the authenticity of messages. The consequences can include financial loss, identity theft, and misuse of personal data.

This case highlights how easily users can be manipulated through urgency and fear. It also shows the importance of being cautious while interacting with unknown links and messages online.
